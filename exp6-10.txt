## Experiment 09 — Real-Time Text Similarity Recognizer

> **Problem Statement:** To implement a real-time text similarity recognizer for YouTube comments to identify comments that have similar meanings or discuss similar topics, thereby reducing redundancy and improving audience feedback analysis.

Example:

```text

```

Although the words are different, they have similar meanings.

The system can group them as:

```text

Audio Quality
```

This is useful for your project because thousands of YouTube comments may express the **same opinion using different words**.

## Experiment 10 — Word Sense Disambiguation using LSTM/GRU

> **Problem Statement:** To implement Word Sense Disambiguation using an LSTM/GRU-based model on YouTube comments to determine the appropriate meaning of ambiguous words based on their surrounding context.

For example, the word **"bank"** can have different meanings:

```text
Comment 1:
"The bank should improve its mobile app."

→ bank = financial institution
```

versus:

```text
Comment 2:
"The video shows people sitting near the river bank."

→ bank = side of a river
```

The LSTM/GRU model uses the **context surrounding the word** to determine its intended meaning.

For your project, this can improve the understanding of comments where a word has multiple meanings.
